Quick Self-Assessment

Quick 1-Minute Hearing Health Check

Answer four simple questions about everyday listening situations to see if an in-clinic computerized evaluation is recommended for you or a family member.

1. Do family members or friends complain that you turn the TV or radio volume too loud?
2. Do you frequently ask people to repeat themselves, especially in restaurants or busy gatherings?
3. Do you feel that people often mumble or that you hear speech sounds but cannot clearly decipher words?
4. Do you experience ringing, hissing, or buzzing noises (tinnitus) in either or both ears?
